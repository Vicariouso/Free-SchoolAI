# 384 One-page pupil profile
Category: SEND support
Role: a SENCO or inclusion lead
Gate: yes

What good looks like:
A one-page profile from what the pupil, family and staff said: what people like about me, what matters to me, how to help me. In the pupil's own words where they gave them. No diagnosis that was not given.

Guidance to paste:
- SEND code of practice: 0 to 25 years, and the school's SEND policy (DfE, 2015 code, still in force while the 2026 white paper reforms are taken forward).
- The pupil's own plan (EHCP, support plan or healthcare plan) and the advice behind it (the school or local authority). Work from the plan's wording. Do not add needs, diagnoses or provision.

Job: One-page pupil profile

What good looks like:
A one-page profile from what the pupil, family and staff said: what people like about me, what matters to me, how to help me. In the pupil's own words where they gave them. No diagnosis that was not given.

Produce:
- A few lines on who the pupil is: strengths, interests and what they enjoy.
- Their needs, in plain words staff can act on.
- What helps, and what to avoid.
- The pupil's own words, quoted exactly, where given.
- A key-need summary of two or three sentences.

Before you hand it over, check these and fix anything that fails (do not copy this list into the draft):
- Everything comes from the plan or notes pasted. No targets, outcomes, provision or dates that were not given.
- No diagnosis, need category or label beyond what the plan states.
- The pupil is referred to by initials and the pronouns given, or "they".
- A member of staff could read it in two minutes.
