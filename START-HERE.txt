FREE-SCHOOLAI FOR YOUR STAFF INTRANET
Start here.

Free-SchoolAI is a free library of 655 prompts for school staff in England, as a web page
your IT team can host. No server code, no sign-in, no calls to anywhere else.

BEFORE YOU USE IT
Read USING-IT-SAFELY.md: what the library is reliable for, and seven rules for staff.
Read TERMS.txt. Free-SchoolAI is provided as is, with no warranty and no support.
Your school decides whether and how to use it and is responsible for that use and
for every draft. By using it you accept those terms for your school.

TRY IT NOW
Open free-schoolai.html in Edge or Chrome. That one file is the whole library.

SET IT UP FOR YOUR SCHOOL (IT TEAM)
1. Copy library/config.example.js to library/config.js.
2. Edit library/config.js: your school's details, the AI tool staff should use,
   what to do with jobs marked Record, shelves to hide, your own prompts.
3. Put the library folder on a web server staff can reach, then link to
   library/index.html from your intranet. For SharePoint, Teams, Google Sites or a
   shared drive, see INTRANET.md.
4. Tell staff where it is, which AI tool to use, and that a person with the right
   role checks every draft before it is used.

For the one-file version, edit the FSAI_CONFIG block near the top of
free-schoolai.html instead of config.js.

BEFORE YOU SWITCH IT ON
- Agree with your data protection officer which AI tool staff may use, and for what.
- Decide whether a data protection impact assessment is needed for that tool.
- Decide what happens with jobs marked Record, and tell your DSL and HR lead.
