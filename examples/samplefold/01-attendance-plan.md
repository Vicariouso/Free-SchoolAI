# Whole-school attendance plan, 2026/27

Fictional sample for Samplefold Community Primary School. Not a real school. A head would still have to check this before governors saw it.

This draft touches attendance. It is not legal advice and it is not Working together to improve school attendance. Figures are invented.

## What the register says

Full year 2025/26. Source in this pack: the school card, treated as the register extract.

Overall attendance was 93.1%. Persistent absence was 18.4%, 57 pupils. Severe absence was 1.6%, 5 pupils. Pupil premium persistent absence was 27%. Year 5 was the lowest year at 91.0%. Term-time leave was 1.1% of sessions, mostly Year 2 and Year 5. Lates were 2.4% of sessions, heaviest on Monday.

The figure to 2 October 2026 is 95.8%. That is three weeks. It is not a year, and this plan does not use it as a result.

No trust target was supplied. This plan does not invent one. The review question is whether persistent absence is lower at the same point next year, and whether Year 5 and pupil premium move with it.

## Barriers

Evidenced in the invented register:

- Term-time leave in Year 2 and Year 5.
- Lates, Monday above other days.

Working view, not evidenced beyond the register pattern:

- The estate bus misses the bell on two mornings. [NEED: which mornings, from the bus company, not from guesswork.]

## Actions

1. Daniel Okoye checks the late list at 9.05 every day. Office calls the first ten unexplained absences before 9.30. Start 6 October 2026. Review 20 October 2026.
2. Year 5 lead brings the 15 lowest Year 5 attenders to a fortnightly 20-minute meeting with Daniel. No new form. Start week of 12 October 2026. Review 7 December 2026.
3. Priya Sen writes to families who took leave in 2025/26 before the October half term, and again in spring before the usual flight weeks. The letter states the school cannot authorise holiday. It does not shame. Owner: head. First letter by 10 October 2026.
4. Office logs the estate bus arrival for ten school days. If it misses the bell, Priya asks the company for the timetable in writing. Owner: office, then head. Log finished 17 October 2026.
5. Pupil premium persistent absence is a standing line in the head's report. Number, not a story. Owner: Daniel. First report 12 November 2026.

Severe absence stays with the head and the local authority link. This plan does not name those five pupils.

## Review

- 20 October 2026: late calls and the bus log.
- 7 December 2026: Year 5 list and persistent absence count.
- 12 November 2026: governors get the numbers, not a new policy.
- July 2027: full-year comparison with 2025/26. Same measure, same codes.

## Assumptions

The school card is the only evidence. Marrowgate and the bus are invented. No code-level extract was supplied, so Monday illness is a working view.
